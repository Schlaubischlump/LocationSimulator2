- First TestFlight release of the rebuilt LocationSimulator for iOS, iPadOS, and Mac.
- Simulate locations on physical iPhones and iPads over USB or Wi-Fi, as well as devices running in iOS Simulator.
- Search for places, enter coordinates, import GPX files, and play walking, cycling, or driving routes.
- Control location workflows with keyboard shortcuts or AppleScript, including an in-app AppleScript API reference.
- Improved device reconnection, route handling, and device-name fallbacks across the app and AppleScript.
