- Updated Refresh and Retry to download and validate replacement files before removing and reinstalling the device’s developer image.
- Updated developer image labels in all supported languages, plus English and German help, FAQs, and Cryptex AppleScript examples.
- Added a device info indicator showing the developer image format currently installed on the device.
- Fixed manual developer image controls disappearing after a failed status refresh, and made refresh failures visible without extending the mount action’s loading indicator.
